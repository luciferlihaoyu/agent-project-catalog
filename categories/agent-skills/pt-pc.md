## 一句话点评
LoreanXavier/pt-pc is a native PC port of the iconic PlayStation game P.T. (Playable Teaser). This preservation project allows PC users to experience Hideo Kojima's legendary silent hill teaser, making an important piece of gaming history accessible beyond the original PlayStation platform.
